How to Read This Report

The order matters more than the sections. Most readers open at the index level and stop there — which is the habit that makes a narrow market look healthy.

1 Start with weekly participation, not the index

Open §7. Which direction is the number of stocks above their 50-day average moving over the last 4 weeks? That is the market’s true direction of travel.

2 Then check the weekly index against it

If the Nifty was flat or mildly green while fewer stocks are above their 20-day average than last week, participation is falling inside a narrow market.

3 Read sector leadership across weekly timeframes

In §6b, compare each sector’s 1-week move against its 1-month and 3-month bars. A sector strong on all three is structural leadership.

4 Cross-check each sector&rsquo;s weekly move against its breadth

A sector that rose this week but whose participation column is falling has a narrow move. §6b row next to §7 row is the comparison.

5 Go straight to the flagged weekly sectors

Read §7’s notable weekly flags and §9’s weekly scanner names together to identify institutional positioning.

6 Ask whether weekly money agrees with positioning

Compare §11 (cumulative weekly FII/DII net flows) with §13 (options put floor and call wall). Conviction shows when both point the same way.
