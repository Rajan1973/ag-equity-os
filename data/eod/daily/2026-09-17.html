GUIDE & METHODOLOGY

How to Read This Report

The order matters more than the sections. Most readers open at the index level and stop there — which is the habit that makes a narrow market look healthy.
Step 1

Start with participation, not the index

Open §7. Which direction is the number of stocks above their 20-day average moving? That is the market’s direction of travel, not the index close.

Step 2

Then check the index against it

If the Nifty rose but fewer stocks are above their 20-day average than yesterday, participation is falling inside a rising index. That divergence is the most important thing on the page.

Step 3

Read sector leadership across timeframes

In §6b, compare each sector’s 1-day move against its 1-week and 1-month bars. A sector strong on the day but weak on the month is a bounce. A sector strong on all three is a trend.

Step 4

Cross-check each sector&rsquo;s move against its own breadth

A sector that rose today but whose participation column is falling has a narrow move — a few stocks doing the work. §6b row next to §7 row is the comparison.

Step 5

Go straight to the flagged sectors

The pack has already joined breadth to the scanners for you. Read §7’s notable flags and §9’s scanner names together — that join is already done.

Step 6

Ask whether the money agrees with the positioning

Compare §11 (what institutions bought and sold) with §13 (where the derivatives positioning sits). Conviction shows when both point the same way.
