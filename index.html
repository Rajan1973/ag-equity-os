DAILY EOD REPORT METHODOLOGY

EOD Report Methodology

Step-by-step institutional framework specifically developed for interpreting the Daily Market Update (EOD) reports, market breadth, and cash flow signals.

ℹ️ Note: This 6-step analytical methodology is specific to interpreting the Daily Market Update (EOD) Reports and their underlying data indicators, rather than general app navigation.

6-Step Analytical Order (EOD Market Reports)

Step 1

Start with Participation, Not Index

Check the percentage of stocks above their 20-day SMA. This reveals the market's true direction of travel underneath the headline.

Step 2

Check Index Against Participation

If the Nifty rises while participation falls, it signals narrow index management driven by a few heavyweights.

Step 3

Read Sector Leadership Across Time

Compare 1-day, 1-week, and 1-month returns. A sector strong on all three frame intervals is in a true trend.

Step 4

Cross-Check Sector Breadth

Verify if a rising sector is supported by rising internal participation rather than single-stock distortions.

Step 5

Scan Stock Anomalies

Review high volume multiples (>3x 63D baseline) to spot institutional accumulation or distribution.

Step 6

Verify Cash Flow Alignment

Compare FII/DII cash market purchases against derivative positioning to confirm institutional conviction.
