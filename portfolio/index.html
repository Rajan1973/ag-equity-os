🏛️ The 7 Input Screener Filters & Consolidated Universe

To eliminate selection bias and emotional stock picking, our entire analysis originated from 7 rigorous fundamental screeners provided by the user:

  1. Companies within 10% of 52W High & 100% from 52W Low: Momentum and technical strength proxy.
  2. FII Buying: Institutional accumulation over consecutive quarters.
  3. Growing Companies with Capacity Addition in Last 2 Years: Real capex and tangible asset creation.
  4. Growth without Dilution: Revenue and profit expansion without equity dilution.
  5. Increasing Quarterly Revenue, PEG & ROCE > 15%: Quality growth at reasonable price.
  6. Quarterly Revenue & Profit Growers (PAT growth > Revenue growth): Operating leverage and gross margin expansion.
  7. Sequential Quarters Revenue and Profit Growers: Consistent QoQ compounding.

Universe Consolidation: Merging these 7 screeners yielded 495 unique Indian listed companies spanning large-caps, mid-caps, and small-caps.

⚖️ The Multi-Factor Scoring Architecture (0–100 Points)

Every stock in the 495 universe was scored across four objective quantitative pillars:

  • Pillar 1: Business Quality & Efficiency (0–30 pts): RoCE > 20% (15 pts), Net Debt/Equity < 0.3 (10 pts), Promoter Holding > 50% (5 pts).
  • Pillar 2: Growth & Operating Momentum (0–30 pts): 3-Year PAT CAGR > 20% (10 pts), Sequential QoQ Revenue Growth (10 pts), Sequential QoQ PAT Growth (10 pts).
  • Pillar 3: Valuation & Margin of Safety (0–25 pts): PEG Ratio < 1.5 (15 pts), P/E vs 3-Yr Median (10 pts).
  • Pillar 4: Institutional Presence & Liquidity (0–15 pts): FII/DII shareholding > 15% (10 pts), Daily Trading Volume (5 pts).

The Core-Satellite Construct: Stocks scoring ≥ 75 pts were nominated for Core (60%), while high-growth emerging companies scoring 65–74 pts formed the Satellite tier (40%).
